The Land

One hundred acres, just north of Young's Point

Mature mixed woodland, wetlands and watercourses, with room to walk for an afternoon.

This is a 103 acre parcel in the Township of Selwyn, north of Young's Point. The property is mixed forest with wetlands and watercourses running through it, and natural trails to follow on foot or by ATV.

Deer and wild turkey are regular visitors. It suits a private hunting property, a recreational retreat, or land held for conservation, and it is a quiet piece of ground within a short drive of Peterborough.
