The Story

Quiet end of a short court, inside the village

A level lot, a cedar hedge along the back, and a house that steps up and down over three levels.

This home sits at the quiet end of a short court inside the village of Lakefield, on a level lot with a cedar hedge along the back and mature trees at the edges. The house was built in 1985, brick across the front with siding above, and it has been kept up and worked on since. It is a side split, so the living space steps up and down over three levels rather than stacking straight above you.

The Main Floor

One space, without feeling like one room

The front door opens into a tiled foyer with the stairs straight ahead. To the right, the living room runs the width of the house, with a bay window looking out over the front lawn and an electric fireplace set into a white shiplap wall. That room opens into the dining area, and the dining area opens into the kitchen, so the whole main floor reads as one space without feeling like a single room.

White cabinets to the ceiling. A navy island with seating for two.

The Kitchen

Redone, and made for company

The kitchen has been redone. White cabinets to the ceiling with glass uppers, a navy island with seating for two, quartz counters, and a white hexagon tile backsplash that carries around the window over the sink. Stainless appliances, and a garden door from the dining area straight out to the patio.

Upstairs

Three bedrooms, up half a flight

The primary takes the back corner and looks over the yard. The main bathroom up there is a five-piece and has been redone as well, with two vessel sinks on an oak vanity, a wall of green glazed tile, and a tub and shower behind glass.

The Lower Level

Room to spread out

Down half a flight from the main floor is the recreation room, with windows at grade along one wall and built-in shelving. There is a fourth bedroom on that level, a two-piece bathroom, laundry, and a walk-in closet, plus a large crawl space under the front of the house for storage.

Patio, hot tub, fire pit. All of it behind a cedar hedge.

The Yard

Patio, hot tub and a fire pit

Outside, a paver patio runs off the dining room doors to a hot tub under a gazebo. Past that the yard opens up: lawn, raised garden beds, a gravel fire pit ring with seating, and a garden shed at the back. The whole yard is hedged. There is an attached garage with inside entry and room for three more vehicles on the drive.

The Neighbourhood

Inside the village

The house is on a court in the village of Lakefield. The street is a dead end with no through traffic, and the ground is level from the road to the back hedge. Houses along the court are detached and date from a similar period, on lots of a comparable size.

This is an in-village address, not an outlying one. The shops, restaurants, grocery store, pharmacy and library of the village core are all close by, on the same side of the river.

The Otonabee River runs through the village, with a marina and a beach nearby, and the Trent-Severn lock at Lakefield. Peterborough is a short drive south for larger shopping, the hospital and the highway network.
